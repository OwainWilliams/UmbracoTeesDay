About

Every now and again the Umbraco community comes together and shares photos of themselves on social media wearing their Umbraco merch.

This can be a t-shirt, socks, hoodies, caps, a t-shirt with an Umbraco sticker on it, anything. They share their photos using the hashtag #umbracoTees.

Pete Duncanson was one of the first to announce an #umbracoTees day. You can read Pete’s reply on why he started it in this blog post about #umbracoTees day.

Where can I get Umbraco merchandise?

There are a number of ways to get your hands on Umbraco merch.

  • Attend an Umbraco conference such as Codegarden, UKFest, DuugFest or Umbraco Spark
  • Attend an Umbraco Meetup, sometimes they have merch to give away
  • Buy some from the Umbraco Community store

Stay in the loop

Add the #UmbracoTees calendar to your calendar app or follow the RSS feed to hear about the next one as soon as it’s announced. Both are on the home page with one-click subscribe links.
